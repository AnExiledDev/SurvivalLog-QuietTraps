# Changelog

## 0.2.0

- The Mods screen links this changelog.
- On the Mods screen, the diagnostics settings sit in the folded Advanced group.
- Settings changed on the Mod Manager's Mods screen take effect at once. A trap that already holds a catch changes once you load a save.
- The Mods screen shows the mod's name, description, category and links.
- Exiled's Toolkit 0.4.0 or newer is optional. With it, settings changed on the Mods screen take effect without a restart; without it, at the next game start.

## 0.1.3

First public release. Mutes the sound a trap keeps playing once it has caught something, with a setting for each catch sound. Every other trap sound still plays.
